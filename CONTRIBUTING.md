# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this learning roadmap.

The topic lists are the planned scope; concept articles will be researched and added one at a time. Please do not treat a checklist item as a completed explanation.

## Quality guidelines

- Keep the stage grouping and priority order consistent with the roadmap unless proposing a clearly explained change.
- Prefer primary sources such as EIPs, protocol specifications, and official documentation.
- Separate specification requirements from client-specific behavior and common practice.
- Write original explanations focused on developer understanding.
- Add references close to the claims they support.
- Label version-, fork-, and implementation-dependent behavior.
- Use diagrams when they genuinely clarify a mechanism; keep editable sources in `assets/diagrams/`.
- Do not represent examples as production-ready or audited unless that is properly established.

## Article conventions

Use [the concept template](templates/concept-template.md) as a starting point and adapt it to the topic. Use numbered stage folders and descriptive lowercase kebab-case filenames if you add a concept file.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The explanation describes what the concept is and why it matters.
- [ ] Technical statements are supported by relevant sources.
- [ ] Version- or fork-dependent behavior is labelled.
- [ ] Code, diagrams, links, and references were checked.
- [ ] Status and review date are accurate.
